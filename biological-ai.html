Research Area · Biological Intelligence

Biological AI

I investigate how living systems can participate directly in computation—not simply as inspiration for algorithms, but as adaptive physical substrates whose native dynamics can transform, store, and process information.

Living computationWet-neuromorphic systemsAdaptive intelligence
Research perspective

What I investigate

My work in Biological AI connects engineered computation with the information-processing capabilities of living systems. The central question is how biological dynamics—gene regulation in bacteria, collective cellular responses, and neuronal network activity—can be organized into useful computational functions while preserving the adaptability and efficiency of the underlying biology.

Living computational substrates

Using biological networks as the physical medium of computation, with emphasis on intrinsic dynamics rather than forcing biology to imitate conventional digital hardware.

Adaptive dynamics

Characterizing how state, history, plasticity, and environmental context change the mapping from input perturbations to biological responses.

Closed-loop interaction

Developing feedback loops in which measured biological activity informs the next stimulation or control action, enabling adaptive biological information processing.

Hybrid bio-digital systems

Combining living systems with lightweight machine-learning readouts, control logic, and digital interfaces to build practical biological computing architectures.

Scientific approach

From biological dynamics to computational function

Research philosophy

The long-term objective is to move from bio-inspired algorithms toward bio-integrated intelligence: computation in which the living system itself provides meaningful nonlinear transformation, memory, and adaptation.

Characterize

Measure the natural operating regimes and response variability of the living system.

Perturb

Apply structured molecular, electrical, or environmental inputs.

Encode

Convert biological responses into computational state representations.

Read out

Use compact statistical or machine-learning layers to map reservoir states to task outputs.

Adapt

Close the loop so stimulation or training can respond to biological state and task performance.

Current & recent work

Research directions

These themes connect mechanistic biological modeling with experimentally grounded computation and lightweight machine-learning readouts.

01

Bacterial intelligence

Gene regulatory networks interpreted as neural-like computing systems for classification, mathematical processing, and signal transformation.

02

Neuronal reservoir computing

Living neuronal cultures used as dynamical reservoirs for nonlinear computation, memory, chaotic-system prediction, and closed-loop control.

03

Microfluidic computation

Exploring physical fluid dynamics and nonlinear mixing as a computation substrate within unconventional reservoir-computing systems.
