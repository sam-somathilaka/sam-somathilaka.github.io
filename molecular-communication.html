Research Area · Communication in Biological Systems

Molecular Communication

I study information transfer through molecules, with particular interest in how communication among cells and microbial communities shapes distributed sensing, coordination, and biological computation.

Molecular signalingMicrobial networksBio-nano communication
Research perspective

What I investigate

Molecular communication treats molecules as information carriers and biological components as transmitters, channels, and receivers. My research examines how these communication processes can be modeled in complex biological environments, how cascaded signaling changes information flow, and how communication can be integrated with computation to support future biological AI and living-device architectures.

Signal propagation

Modeling how molecular signals are generated, transported, transformed, and detected across biological communication pathways.

Networked microbiomes

Representing large microbial ecosystems as communication graphs in which topology and species interactions influence information flow.

Cascaded communication

Analyzing multi-stage molecular communication systems where downstream nodes amplify, relay, or reshape a signal.

Communication-computation integration

Studying molecular communication not only as transport, but as part of distributed biological information processing and machine learning.

Scientific approach

From biological dynamics to computational function

Research philosophy

In living systems, communication and computation are deeply coupled: the same molecular interactions that carry information can also transform it. Understanding that coupling is central to scalable biological intelligence.

Source

Define the biological transmitter and how information is encoded into molecular signals.

Channel

Model propagation, diffusion, reaction, amplification, and interaction with the surrounding biological environment.

Receiver

Characterize how a cell or molecular network senses and transforms the arriving signal.

Network

Extend point-to-point models to cascades, communities, and graph-structured biological systems.

Computation

Investigate how network communication can support learning, inference, and coordinated biological function.
